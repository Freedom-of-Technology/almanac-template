# An email address stopped working

Use this when mail to one of your business's addresses bounces or never arrives, for example
someone tells you their email to isthisreal@freedomoftechnology.com came back. The list of addresses, and
where each one's mail should go, is in [Your email addresses](../workspace/addresses.md).

If **all** your email stopped working, not just one address, use
[Email stopped working](email-stopped-working.md) instead.

## 1. Find the address in your list

Open [Your email addresses](../workspace/addresses.md) and find the address in the table.
"Where its mail goes" says whose mailbox it should arrive in, and "Set up as" says how it was
made.

If it isn't in the table, it was never written down: contact FreeOT (below) and say which
address.

## 2. Check it's still set up, and put it back

Your list says which company runs your email. Under **Putting an address back**, it gives that
company's steps for checking an address is still there and adding it again if it's gone.
Follow them.

If your list says to contact FreeOT instead, do that, and say which address.

## 3. Test it

From a different email account, such as a personal one, send a short email to the address.
Check that it arrives in the right mailbox. If it still doesn't arrive after an hour, contact
FreeOT (below) and say which address, and when you sent the test.

## If you didn't remove it

If an address disappeared and nobody you know removed it, someone else may have had access to
your email settings. Before anything else, change the password of every account that can
change them, then contact FreeOT.

## Getting help

Contact FreeOT at almanac-check@freedomoftechnology.com. Say which guide you were on and what you saw.
