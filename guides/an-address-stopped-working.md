# An email address stopped working

Use this when mail to one of your business's addresses bounces or never arrives, for example
someone tells you their email to isthisreal@freedomoftechnology.com came back. The list of addresses, and
where each one's mail should go, is in [Your email addresses](../workspace/addresses.md).

If **all** your email stopped working, not just one address, use
[Email stopped working](email-stopped-working.md) instead.

## 1. Check the address is still set up

1. Go to the Google Admin console: https://admin.google.com. Sign in with your own business
   account.
2. On the left, click **Directory**, then **Users**.
3. Click the name of the person whose mailbox the address goes to. [Your email
   addresses](../workspace/addresses.md) says whose it is, in "Where its mail goes".
4. Click **User information**. Under **Alternate email addresses (email alias)**, look for the
   address.

If it's there, skip to step 3 below. If it's missing, put it back.

## 2. Put it back

1. On the same person's page, in the list of actions on the left, click **ADD ALTERNATE
   EMAILS**.
2. In the **Alternate email** box, type the part of the address before the `@`, for example
   `isthisreal`. Leave the domain next to it as it is: your main domain. If your business has a
   second domain set up as another name for it, the address works there too, without adding it
   again ([Your email addresses](../workspace/addresses.md) says whether yours does).
3. Click **SAVE**.

Google says a new address can take a little while to start working.

## 3. Test it

From a different email account, such as a personal one, send a short email to the address.
Check that it arrives in the right mailbox. If it still doesn't arrive after an hour, contact
FreeOT (below) and say which address, and when you sent the test.

## If you didn't remove it

If an address disappeared and nobody you know removed it, someone else may have had access to
your Google Workspace. Before anything else, change the password of every admin account, then
contact FreeOT.

## Getting help

Contact FreeOT at almanac-check@freedomoftechnology.com. Say which guide you were on and what you saw.
